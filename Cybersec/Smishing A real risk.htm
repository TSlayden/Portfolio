“Smishing? What the heck is that? You made up that word, Tim”

 

Nope, it’s a real thing. In the cybersecurity world Smishing is a term for Phishing over text messaging (SMS)

 

SMS + Phishing = Smishing

 

Be on guard against fake shipping notifications sent via text. Bad actors know you are trying to complete your holiday shopping. They are using fake delivery notifications to prey on the sense of urgency surrounding these orders arriving in a timely manner.

 

Pay attention to links contained in shipping notifications. If the web domain is not EXACTLY a major carrier then they are FAKE. (ups.com , usps.com , fedex.com)
