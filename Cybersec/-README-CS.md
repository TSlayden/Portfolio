Security education is the frontline of defense in the modern workplace. This repository is a collection of internal documents to reinforce the security mindset and incident response readiness. Of particular note is the Spear Phish Presentation, compiled following a targeted phishing incident involving the company President and an employee. Also included is the data restoration manual detailing recovery procedures across multiple storage environments.

-Restore Manual
Standard operating procedure for recovering files from various company storage devices. Outlines step-by-step instructions for restoring data from appliance snapshots and dedicated backup hardware.

-Spear Phish Presentation
A post-incident review and educational presentation compiled following a targeted phishing attack on company personnel. It details the mechanics of the incident, a model of how it may have occurred, and proactive steps employees can take to recognize and mitigate similar threats.

-Be Wary of Email Links
A phishing prevention communication focused on identifying fraudulent IT support alerts. This specifically addresses "quishing" (QR code phishing) attempts designed to harvest credentials via spoofed login portals.

-Phishing Communication
An organization-wide communication educating staff on the broad spectrum of email-based attack vectors deployed by threat actors.

-Smishing: A Real Risk
An awareness communication targeting SMS-based phishing (smishing). This communication highlights seasonal threats, such as fraudulent holiday shipping notifications, designed to trick users into divulging credentials.
